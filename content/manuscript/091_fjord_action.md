The ageing smoke machine belched erratic clouds of whitish muck over the expectant and fidgety crowd. Dora motioned to Molly to hustle to the front of the stage, as she saw the sound engineer twiddle the last knobs on the guitar amp, and he bustled off-stage, a sure sign things were about to blast off. The famous Houtrakkers, or as their stage name was, 2BoN2B, and she was dying to see them outside of their hidey-hole. Were they even of flesh and blood? Hard to tell...

Mr. Ate took to the stage as the crowd yelled and catcalled. "Now announcing the moment you've all been waiting for... I proudly present the best punk rock band since Sonic Youth, 2BoN2B!!!" 

The stage was already hard to make out because of the smoke, now the lights went full on red, and the smoke machine full blast - too much for its antiquated circuitry, it roared, belched, and sparked and misted like Krakatoa and the ensuing short-circuit blew the lights - the crowd was in dismay and it was utter pitch black - Dora could only hear a swish and imagined a residual retina imprint of Mr. Ate shooing off pronto to the left of the stage. 

"Where's he going? Molly, where is the band - Molly?" She grasped in the blind to her right, where Molly had been. There was no Molly beside her, only a tatty woolly sweater.

A gravely voice said, "Yo I'm no Molly, damn it's dark," and the owner of the voice lit a lighter, as did others in the crowd. At the bar in the back a flashlight was on and someone was rummaging for matches to promptly light some emergency candles. Dora sped round on her axis, neither Molly nor Mr. Ate were anywhere to be seen. 

"WTF," she exclaimed, and grabbed for her bike light deep in her jeans pocket. "Where are they? The john, most likely," and she ran onto the stage, tripping over the spaghetti of cables and falling over the crouching sound engineer who lamented the demise of the mist machine. She tumbled down the left of the stage, through the curtains and suddenly remembered the Cold War bomb shelter tunnel access. _That must be where they went, though WTF, WTF, WTF_ she thought, and felt around in the dark until she came to the cold steel door, and pushed down hard on the blast door handle and it swung open to a dark gray dimness. Dora suddenly grew very cold, gripped by fear like tight cold hand constricting her throat and chest. _What if Molly ran off with Mr. Ate_ she thought and stumbled on into the tunnel which had periodic old sodium lamps flickering dimly so she could see her way around. She moved into the hallway stretching back quite far into the gloom. She couldn't remember reading how far the tunnels reached. 

She heard footsteps approaching behind her a passageway to her right, and she saw a doorway to her left where it was dark. She ran in and hid behind the door, just able to see part of the corridor. She saw a pair of legs and booted feet sneak past. It wasn't Mr. Ate, nor Molly - it was the hairy punk who had stood right next to her. _What a coincidence, she thought. Or was it?_ 

He carried a powerful flashlight and its narrow beam was almost blinding. He appeared to look for footsteps in the dust. _OMG hope he won't find me,_ thought Dora, holding her breath. But on he went. He suddenly looked up, as if he heard a noise further down the corridor and he snuck thataway quickly. Dora decided to follow him, albeit at a safe distance, staying a corner behind so as to avoid being spotted if he happened to look behind im. 

Then she heard a loud bang and an oomph! and a thud. She glanced around the corner and saw what looked like Mr. Ate dragging the punk into a doorway and closing it. _WTF was this all about!_ Dora decided this was too much for her and she darted back into the stage where the lights appeared on again. She went to the loo, kind of in consternation, where she overheard that the local band would set up some karaoke to pass the time and have some well-deserved entertainment.
