
# Acknowledgments

The idea for *The Blackout: Weak Signals* came into my head when I was commuting from Haarlem to my work in Amsterdam Sloterdijk somewhere between 2010 and 2014. Yes, near the Vinkebrug...

The idea greatly appealed to me, yet it seemed daunting how to turn that idea (which is the key of chapter one) into anything more robust. 

In the summer of 2025 a sentence popped into my head and didn't let me go. It took me a while to realise that it was in fact the first sentence for that idea. And it still is!

This book was written on an Olivetti mechanical typewriter of the model Dora. Total coincidence.

I would like to thank my proofreaders who gave me invaluable corrections where my science, biochemistry, ornithology, psychology and storyline was errant: 

Nora Ghaoui, Jet de Waij, Laura Koopmans, Niels Remmelzwaal, Kees Alders, Paul Schendelaar...

And my most patient love of my life, Maartje Kniest. 

—
F. J. S. Remmelzwaal
