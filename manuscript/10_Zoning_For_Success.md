Of course! That’s why it had been so quiet in the streets. That’s why they had been able to evade, duck, dive, and hide so effectively. The better half of Schoten had been flattened in a drug-induced fugue, squalor, hibernation.

And that’s why everyone in the train was more doped up than usual.
