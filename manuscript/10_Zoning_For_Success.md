She surreptitiously scanned her fellow commuters’ countenances — were they really all that amnesiac? How entirely effective the combination of propaganda, emotional dampening, structural attention-draining, retro-wiping, and mild malnutrition were to keep the man down.

Barely a week ago, the train had been filled with thousands of other people — these gits were their replacements! And they were as sweet as they come, as meek as lambs. Casually scrolling the news bulletins and regurgitated doomscrolling drivel, as if they weren’t literally building on the bones, riding over the cremated accordion of thousands of lives. Surely, some of them must have had relatives. Surely, some must feel a deep sense of loss, and guilt at taking their place.

Yes, there had been a period of mourning. With the invitation to every funeral came a small package of government-prescribed _Painquell_ — five capsules that would knock your socks off and fry your memory. You’d only wake for a glass of water and a capsule once every twenty-four hours.

Of course! That’s why it had been so quiet in the streets. That’s why they had been able to evade, duck, dive, and hide so effectively. The better half of Schoten had been flattened in a drug-induced stupor.

And that’s why everyone in the train was more doped up than usual.

## Phil

Dora exited the SubT one stop earlier, Spierinxhoorn, where, just opposite the soccer fields, she was greeted with an iron gate picturing Our Dear Leader, Dustin, beckoning the proles from the past to step into the glorious future of the Free Economic Era. She wanted to walk the last two clicks and take the time to anticipate on how her comrades might see her with suspicion and anticipate any insiduous remarks with a glib retort. 

Underlying that resolve, was her fervent hope that the Houtrakkers could be relied on to have informed her workplace and chef Phil that her absence was indeed credible and justified.

Phil Cortana, that conniving, wormtongue mouthbreather of a creature, wasn't easily duped. He had been on her case since last year at the XDay party – hunching too close to her, his vile breath from a long-neglected halitosis curdling the very air, his eyes bloodshot from the nitrogen dioxide emissions, and he threatened, "My department is short on  Neg Points, ms. Olivetti, so just beware, I might need to push you out of the Zone of Success and into Reticulating Splines!" Dora shuddered at the memory, and the blustery wind grew in strength and blew wetly from the West through her parka.

Reticulating Splines was a re-integration, or demotion, assigned to the twenty-second floor, the Testing Floor. It was one step removed from being sent-off with other prominent intelligentia to Chinese Congo and shoveling lithium and cadmium in open-pit mines. It was dangerous to appear a know-it-all, and Dora, Erwin and Tricia dumbed down their speaking and even removed their glasses in public when they could.
